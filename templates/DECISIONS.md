# Decisions

Append-only record of key decisions and why they were made. Never edit a past entry: if a decision changes, add a new entry that supersedes it and say which one. Newest at the top, so the current thinking is visible without scrolling through the history.

## Format

```
## YYYY-MM-DD [n] Short decision title
**Decision:** What was chosen.
**Why:** The reasoning, what drove it.
**Alternatives considered:** What was rejected and why (optional).
**Supersedes:** the date and title of an earlier decision this replaces (optional).
```

`[n]` is a per-date sequence number, so two decisions on one day stay in order.
